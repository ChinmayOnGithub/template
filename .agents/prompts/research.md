# Technical and Market Research Prompt

Use this prompt when evaluating unknown technologies, comparing open-source libraries, or analyzing competitors.

## Task
Conduct structured research using primary sources and produce an actionable decision recommendation.

## Steps
1. **State the Research Question**:
   - Exactly what unknown or tradeoff must be resolved?
2. **Consult Primary Sources**:
   - Inspect official documentation, release notes, GitHub source code, and RFCs.
   - Avoid low-quality SEO articles and unverified summaries.
3. **Compare Alternatives**:
   - Populate comparison matrix: bundle impact, latency, ergonomics, maintenance health, license compatibility.
4. **Synthesize Findings**:
   - Recommended approach with concrete technical justification.
   - Risks, tradeoffs, and mitigation plan.
   - Record in `docs/research/RESEARCH.md`.

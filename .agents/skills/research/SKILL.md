# Research Skill

Use this workflow when evaluating new technologies, comparing open-source solutions, or exploring unfamiliar domains.

## 1. Research Protocol
1. **Define the Question**: State the exact unknown (e.g. "Best lightweight SQLite ORM for local-first Bun apps").
2. **Search Primary Documentation**: Prioritize official docs, RFCs, and source repos over SEO blog posts.
3. **Analyze Prior Art & Competitors**: How do market leaders solve this? Where do users get frustrated?
4. **Evaluate Tradeoffs**: Analyze latency, bundle impact, developer ergonomics, maintenance health, and license.
